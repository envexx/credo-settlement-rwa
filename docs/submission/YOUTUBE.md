## Title

Credo — Move RWA Ownership with Verifiable Payment | Creditcoin × Attestcoin

## Description

Credo is a proof-triggered RWA settlement layer for Creditcoin.

A buyer pays official USDC on Ethereum Sepolia. Attestcoin turns the payment receipt into cryptographic evidence. The Creditcoin contract verifies that proof on-chain and releases the ERC-1155 RWA only when the buyer, recipient, token, amount, and payment window match exactly.

No bridge. No wrapped USDC. No trusted worker. The payment stays final on Ethereum, the asset stays native on Creditcoin, and only proof crosses chains.
